StartingLine

Start before the habits do

StartingLine is a phone habits app. Your teen sets limits on their phone. You get a parent view so you can stay involved without reading their messages.

For families handing over a first phone.

They run the controls. You help shape the plan.

On their phone, StartingLine lets teens pick which apps get limited and what parents can see. Parents get weekly check-ins on how those limits went. Messages and private chats stay private.

How it works

Set it up once on their phone. Then use the weekly check-ins to talk it through.

  1. Sit down together

    Open the app on their phone. Your teen chooses which apps to limit and what shows up in your parent view. You give input. They confirm.

  2. They own the goals

    Limits come from goals they write in the app, not a control panel sitting on your phone.

  3. Check in each week

    StartingLine shows what they stuck to and what slipped, so you have a clear report to talk about together.

Two seats. One agreement.

Your teen uses the app on their phone. You use the parent view.

Your teen

  • Sets goals and which apps to limit
  • Chooses what the parent view can show
  • Practices sticking to their own rules

You

  • Help shape the plan at setup
  • See progress against their goals, not their messages
  • Use the weekly report for follow-up

What we never touch

StartingLine does not read messages or open private chats. It tracks the phone limits you agreed on at home, nothing more.
