StartingLine

Start before the habits do

For families with a first phone on the way.

Your teen sets the limits

They choose what’s blocked and what you can see. You guide from beside them.

Your teen builds phone habits on their own terms. You show up as a partner, not a watchdog. We surface the moments worth talking about. Wins included. Messages stay private.

How it works

One sitting to set things up. Then check-ins that feel useful.

  1. Sit down together

    Your teen walks through what’s blocked and what you can see. You advise. They press the buttons.

  2. They own the goals

    Limits come from commitments they wrote down, not a lecture from your phone.

  3. Talk about the moments

    Weekly check-ins show what improved and what slipped. Progress gets airtime too.

Two seats. One agreement.

Clear roles. No reading their texts.

Your teen

  • Sets goals and block lists
  • Chooses what parents can see
  • Learns to manage their habits

You

  • Guide and correct when needed
  • See the shared picture, not their messages
  • Celebrate real improvement

What we never touch

No reading messages. No private chats. StartingLine is about habits and home agreements, not surveillance.
